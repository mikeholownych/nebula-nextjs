Visitor re-qualifies the page. Bounce.

**Why it costs money:**
Bounce rate +12%
Bounce rate × (average traffic) × (average visitor value) = silent revenue leak
For most founders: $500–$1,400/month

**The Fix (copy-paste ready):**

1. Open your landing page
2. Look at your #1 ad
3. Copy the headline from the ad
4. Paste it into your H1
5. Test for 7 days
6. Measure bounce rate (Google Analytics → Behavior → Landing Pages)

Not "Compare rates" or "Quick quotes online" - the EXACT phrase from the ad.
