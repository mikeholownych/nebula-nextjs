# Why is paid traffic not converting?

Paid traffic often fails after the click because the page does not continue the promise that earned the click. Check message match, trust, mobile clarity, and the next action before changing the campaign. This answer is a practical diagnostic starting point, not a claim about a specific customer or a guaranteed result.

## What should I inspect first after a paid click?

Start with the first screen. It should repeat the ad's problem, audience, and promised outcome in plain language. Then identify whether the primary action is visible, specific, and possible without unnecessary friction.

## Why does message match matter?

A visitor who clicked for one promise should immediately see that promise reflected in the headline and supporting proof. When the page changes the subject, the visitor has to reconstruct the offer and may leave before understanding it.

## What evidence can I collect without guessing?

Record the ad language, landing-page headline, first call to action, visible trust signals, and mobile layout. Nebula's [audit method](/how-nebula-audits) treats these as inspectable page conditions. Compare the written promise with rendered evidence, and label assumptions as assumptions.

## What questions does this FAQ answer?

### Is low conversion always an ad problem?

No. The ad can attract the right click while the landing page loses continuity, clarity, or trust. Inspect both surfaces before changing spend.

### Should I change the campaign before the page?

Not when the page fails to restate the click promise. Verify the page condition first, then make the smallest evidence-backed change.
