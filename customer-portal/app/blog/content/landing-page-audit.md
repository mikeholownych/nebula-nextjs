# What is a landing page audit and what does it check?

<!-- DRAFT SCAFFOLD -- REQUIRES CTW REVIEW BEFORE PUBLICATION -->
<!-- Brief: content-ledger/briefs/landing-page-audit.json -->
<!-- Evidence verified: nebula-audit-db-2026-09 (892 audits), nebula-audit-method (Citable v1.14.0, 123 detectors) -->

A landing page audit is a structured diagnostic that identifies specific, measurable conditions on a page that reduce conversion. It is not a score. It is not a letter grade. It is a prioritized list of findings, each tied to an observable condition and a known impact on visitor behavior. In Nebula's database of 892 completed audits, the most common finding category is above-the-fold layout (21.3% of all issues, average severity 3.6 out of 5), not headline quality or CTA placement, which are the two areas most CRO advice focuses on.

[EXPAND: SEO audit addresses crawlability, indexation, content structure, links. Landing page audit addresses visitor experience and conversion. Overlap exists (load speed, heading structure) but the primary questions are different. Both are worth running; they are not substitutes.]
