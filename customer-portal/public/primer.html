10 common questions

Will this work if I'm not running paid ads yet?
Yes. Organic traffic from SEO, Reddit, LinkedIn, or email has the same problem: if the page doesn't convert, the traffic is wasted. Fixing the conversion rate before scaling traffic is the highest-leverage move you can make — whether you're spending on ads or not.
What platforms do you support?
The audit works on any public URL. The AI prompts are platform-agnostic — paste them into Claude or ChatGPT, or hand them to whoever maintains your Webflow, Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace, or custom-coded page. We don't touch your platform directly, so there's nothing we can't support.
What if my page only scores a 7 or above?
If every signal already scores 7 or higher, email us before buying — we'll tell you honestly whether there's anything in the pack worth $97, rather than take your money for something that won't add value. That said, most pages score below 7 in at least one signal that's actually costing conversions.
Do I need to give you access to my site or codebase?
No. The audit reads your page's public HTML only — never your analytics, CMS, ad accounts, or server. The Fix Pack is a written diagnosis plus AI prompts; nothing is pushed to your site by us.
How is this different from running it through a free tool like PageSpeed Insights?
PageSpeed Insights tells you what's slow. It doesn't tell you why your headline isn't connecting, why your CTA is getting ignored, or why visitors are scrolling past your social proof. We look at the conversion psychology of the page — not just technical metrics — and give you a ready-to-use AI prompt to fix it, not just the score.
Is there a call involved?
No call. No calendar. No Zoom. Run the audit, read your results, buy the Fix Pack if you want the prompts. If you have a question, email is fine.
What does "delivered within minutes" actually mean?
Within minutes of payment, you get an email with the full findings and a complete AI prompt pack — one prompt per weak signal, built from your actual page. No 24-hour wait, no queue.
What if a prompt is just wrong for my page?
Email us and tell us what's off. We'll look at it case by case — there's no fixed refund policy to point at, just a real person reading what you send.
Can you fix my whole website, not just one page?
The $97 Fix Pack covers one landing page. If you want multi-page coverage, ask about the retainer.
I already had an agency audit my site. Why would this be different?
Agency audits produce decks that tell you to "think about" fixing things. We give you a ready-to-paste AI prompt to fix it yourself, today — there's no implementation phase to wait on, because you're not waiting on us to implement anything.
