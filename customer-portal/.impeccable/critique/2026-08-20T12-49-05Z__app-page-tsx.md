### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment:** Partially grounded with template relics. Nebula's core visual thesis—clinical, forensic diagnostic authority—is powerful where it leans into verifiable DOM evidence (CSS selectors, pixel coordinates, evidence boundaries). However, the hero section introduces architectural tension: it borrows centered template patterns, decorative waveform curves (`SignalHorizon`), and an artificial 2.6-second simulated scanning delay that conflicts with Nebula's "Evidence over opinion" brand pillar. Furthermore, high-density diagnostic components (`AuditCardArtifact`, `ROICalculator`, `HowItWorksAnimated`) have been decoupled from the homepage in favor of an 896px scaled desktop mockup.

**Deterministic Scan:** `node .agents/skills/impeccable/scripts/detect.mjs --json app/ components/` exited with code 0 (0 automated violations). Ast/token verification confirmed GeistSans/GeistMono compliance, zero layout-property transitions, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` coverage. However, forensic analysis flagged two quality issues:
1. `--fg-dim: #525750` on `--bg: #080909` calculates to **2.8:1 contrast** (fails WCAG AA 4.5:1 on 11-12px microcopy).
2. `.heading-display` tracking is `-0.05em` (exceeds the `-0.04em` craft floor).

---

### Overall Impression
Nebula has an authoritative, high-integrity product thesis with exceptional evidence boundaries. The lower sections (teardowns, $97 sprint, and what the audit cannot claim) build immense trust. The primary opportunity is to declutter the hero fold: eliminate the fake scan delay, resolve competing CTA mechanisms, lift muted text contrast, and re-anchor the layout around an interactive audit card artifact.

---

### What's Working
1. **Clinical Evidence Boundaries:** Section 6 ("What the audit can prove. What it cannot.") sets an industry standard for honesty, disclaiming revenue guarantees and sales calls to establish instant credibility.
2. **Forensic Selector Grounding:** Real CSS selectors (`button#checkout-cta`), rule IDs, and pixel measurements replace generic CRO advice.
3. **Transparent Commercial Architecture:** The $97 One-Leak Repair Sprint is clearly framed as a bounded 48-hour self-implementation kit with a 30-day re-audit.

---

### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Hero Decision Paralysis & Action Competition**
  - **What:** The hero presents three separate action mechanisms simultaneously: (1) URL input pill with submit arrow, (2) "Run Free Audit" CTA button, and (3) "Explore Teardowns" secondary button, alongside an interactive dashboard mockup.
  - **Why it matters:** Violates cognitive load limits (5 competing interactive choices) and dilutes the primary funnel.
  - **Fix:** Unify the hero into a single interaction: integrate the "Run Free Audit" action directly into the URL pill, and demote secondary exploration to a subtle anchor.
  - **Suggested Command:** `$impeccable layout`

- **[P1] Sub-AA Contrast on Muted Microcopy (`--fg-dim`)**
  - **What:** `--fg-dim: #525750` on `--bg: #080909` yields a **2.8:1** contrast ratio on trust chips (11px) and signal requirement notes (12px).
  - **Why it matters:** Fails WCAG AA (4.5:1), making essential verification details hard to read on mobile screens and in ambient light.
  - **Fix:** Promote `--fg-dim` microcopy to `--fg-muted` (`#7a8078`, 5.4:1) or `#9e9e9e` (7.8:1).
  - **Suggested Command:** `$impeccable colorize`

- **[P1] Artificial "Scanning" Delay Undermines Forensic Authority**
  - **What:** `HeroSection.tsx` runs a 2.6-second `setInterval` loop cycling through fake scanning text before redirecting.
  - **Why it matters:** Operators recognize client-side timers as "AI theater," directly contradicting the brand promise of clinical honesty.
  - **Fix:** Remove the fake delay. Validate the URL and immediately route to `/audit?url=...` where the live audit engine streams genuine progress.
  - **Suggested Command:** `$impeccable distill`

- **[P2] Alignment with Asymmetric Split in DESIGN.md**
  - **What:** `DESIGN.md` Section 5 specifies an asymmetric 55/45 split (headline/CTA left, composed audit card right). The hero currently uses a centered stack with an 896px scaled mockup.
  - **Why it matters:** Pushes visual evidence below the fold on standard laptops (1366x768 / 1440x900).
  - **Fix:** Refactor desktop hero to a 2-column grid with live `AuditCardArtifact` on the right.
  - **Suggested Command:** `$impeccable shape`

- **[P2] Internal Jargon in Eyebrows and Status Badges**
  - **What:** Hero uses insular terms: "9 Governed Signals", "Deterministic Priority Ranking", "Preserving evidence atoms".
  - **Why it matters:** Adds extraneous cognitive load for first-time founders seeking conversion leak detection.
  - **Fix:** Replace with outcome-driven language: "9 Conversion Leak Checks", "Direct HTML Inspection", "Ranked by Priority".
  - **Suggested Command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (Impatient Power User):** Forced to wait through a 2.6s fake scan animation with no bypass or keyboard shortcut (`/` or `Cmd+K`) to submit directly.
- **Jordan (Confused First-Timer):** Overwhelmed by 5 competing interactive paths in the hero; encounters engineering terms ("deterministic ranking", "evidence atoms") without inline definitions.
- **Casey (Distracted Mobile User):** Hero element stack is vertically elongated; 40x40px submit arrow on far right edge causes thumb strain during one-handed mobile use.

### Minor Observations

- `.heading-display` tracking is `-0.05em` (exceeds `-0.04em` floor).
- Aphoristic rebuttal rhythm ("This is the output. Not a pitch deck.") repeats across 5 consecutive sections.
