### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment:** Significantly improved and well-aligned with Nebula's clinical forensic authority. Removing the 2.6s artificial scan delay in favor of direct, sanitized routing to `/audit?url=...` replaces agency theatrics with authentic instrument-grade responsiveness. Consolidating the hero action into a single pill-shaped input with an embedded "Run Free Audit" button and demoting secondary discovery to a restrained link ("or explore verified public teardowns ->") strips out clutter and establishes immediate operational intent. Tightened heading tracking (`-0.035em`) and high-contrast microcopy (`--fg-dim: #8b9489`, 5.6:1) complete the refined bench-instrument aesthetic.

### Priority Issues

- **[P2] Mobile Input Optimization & Empty Submit Focus**
  - **What:** `<input id="hero-landing-url">` lacks mobile optimization attributes (`type="url"`, `inputMode="url"`, `autoCapitalize="none"`), and clicking submit on an empty input does not focus the field.
  - **Why it matters:** On mobile devices, default keyboards may pop up with capitalization or standard spacebar layout rather than URL-optimized keys (`.com`, `/`).
  - **Fix:** Add `type="url"`, `inputMode="url"`, `autoCapitalize="none"`, `autoCorrect="off"`, and auto-focus the input on empty submit.
  - **Suggested Command:** `$impeccable polish`

- **[P3] Documentation Signal Count Synchronization**
  - **What:** `PRODUCT.md` mentions "seven conversion signals" in sections 17 & 31, while `page.tsx` and `HeroSection.tsx` implement 9 signals.
  - **Why it matters:** Internal documentation drift.
  - **Fix:** Update `PRODUCT.md` signal references to 9 signals.
  - **Suggested Command:** `$impeccable document`
