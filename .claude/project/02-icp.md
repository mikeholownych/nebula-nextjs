# Nebula Components — ICP

## The struggling moment (primary filter)

Not a demographic. An event.

A founder opens Stripe on a Tuesday morning. Ads are running. Clicks are arriving. Revenue is zero. The page looks fine. They don't know where the leak is. The longer they wait, the more money disappears.

**That specific event is the filter. Everything else is secondary.**

## In — qualifying signals

Use these to determine if a prospect has the struggling moment:

- "I've been running Facebook/Google Ads for X weeks and no sales"
- "We get traffic but no one converts"
- "My ROAS went from 3x to 0.8x and I don't know why"
- "I'm spending $50/day, getting clicks, maybe 2-3 sales a week"
- "We launched paid search but the CPC is eating our budget"
- Any variation of: active ad spend + zero or near-zero conversion + confusion about why

Behavioral evidence (strongest signals):
- Has spent real money on paid traffic (not planning to)
- Has a live landing page or offer page right now
- Has already tried tweaking the page themselves
- Is 1-3 months into the problem (not brand new, not totally defeated)

## Out — disqualifying signals (fast-exit)

- Agencies selling ads or CRO (wrong buyer — unless Agency Partner)
- Founders asking abstract marketing questions with no spend or URL
- "We're planning to launch ads next month" — no struggling moment yet
- "We want to optimize our site" — vague intent, no urgency
- Free-tool promoters and roast-thread tourists
- Anyone who wants a call before seeing the audit output

## Role

Founder, co-founder, or solo operator who:
- Is personally running or directly overseeing paid acquisition
- Has decision-making authority to buy a $97 product without approval
- Is not delegating this to a marketing hire

## Company size

1-10 people. Founder still involved in growth decisions. Not a corporate marketing department.

## Budget signal

Is spending real money on ads. $20-$5,000/month. Enough that zero conversion feels painful.

## Disqualifiers

- Agencies: want to own the fix, not receive a kit
- Consultants: will critique the audit, not act on it
- Already working with a CRO agency: too many cooks
- Company size >50: procurement process, not a $97 decision
- No ad spend: no urgency, no pain

## Pipeline state flow

discovered → site_found → contacted → audit_delivered → pitch_sent → paid
Terminal states: dead, bounced, max_retries_exceeded
