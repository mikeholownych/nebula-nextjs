# /compose — Write Outbound Message

Rules:
1. Load Voice DNA first. Read all sections.
2. Load the prospect's buying trigger signal.
3. Load the prospect's ICP fit score.
4. If < 24 hours since last touch, skip.
5. Open with their specific pain (quote their comment or ad symptom).
6. State the diagnosis you found (or can find with the audit).
7. CTA: the free audit URL.
8. Max 150 words for DM, 200 for email.
9. Check for banned words. If present, rewrite.
10. Run the vague-to-specific pass before sending:
   - Flag generic claims such as "improve conversions," "save money," "grow your business," or "get better results."
   - Rewrite each flagged claim into three alternatives grounded in the prospect's observed symptom, likely business consequence, and concrete next step.
   - Use only evidence available from the prospect's post, ad symptom, public page, or audit. Never invent savings, conversion lifts, revenue, or urgency.
   - Keep the emotional consequence specific but respectful: show what the leak costs in attention, wasted clicks, missed enquiries, or decision friction.
   - Select the strongest version that preserves the original message and fits the one-CTA limit.
11. Read aloud. If it sounds like a template, delete and restart.
