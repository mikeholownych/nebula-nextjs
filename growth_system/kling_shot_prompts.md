## What Each Clause Does (audit reference)

| Clause | Job | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|
| Subject in motion | Name a verb, not a noun. "Types a URL slowly" not "a keyboard" | "A monitor screen" — no motion |
| Environment + light | Anchor the world the motion lives in | Skipped entirely, model invents generic office |
| Camera move + lens | Tell the camera what to do + what kind of camera | Written into the subject clause instead |
| Style anchor | Lock the look across all shots | Forgotten on one shot = visual whiplash |

## Prohibited words (Nebula prompts)
cinematic · epic · beautiful · aesthetic · stunning · amazing · professional
→ Replace with camera/lens nouns. "35mm anamorphic" is signal. "Cinematic" is noise.
