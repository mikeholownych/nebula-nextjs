# The Offer Constructor

**Job:** Sequence Builder  
**Profile:** `growth`  
**Purpose:** sharpens what you are actually asking for, because most sequences fail on a vague ask rather than bad copy.

## Prompt template

My ask is probably too vague. Fix it.

WHAT I CURRENTLY ASK FOR: [PASTE THE EXACT WORDS]
WHO I ASK: [ROLE]
WHAT I ACTUALLY WANT LONG TERM: [THE REAL GOAL]

Return:

WHAT IS WRONG WITH MY ASK
Specifically. Is it too big, too vague, too early, or does it ask them to do
the work of figuring out what I mean.

THE LADDER
Five asks for the same person, smallest to largest. Each one a real, concrete
action with a clear yes or no.

THE RIGHT RUNG
Which one to open with given how cold they are, and why.

THE REWRITE
My ask, rewritten at that rung, in under 15 words.

Rules:
- "Do you have 15 minutes" is not an ask, it is a request for a favour. If my
  version is that, say so bluntly.
- The smallest rung should be answerable with one word.

## Watch for

your own attachment to the meeting ask. The smaller rung usually outperforms it.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
