# Trend Research

**Job:** Signal Hunter  
**Profile:** `market`  
**Purpose:** takes a topic and comes back with what genuinely changed in the last thirty days, with sources, so your opener references something real instead of something generic.

## Prompt template

Research what actually changed in [TOPIC] over the last 30 days.

I need this for cold outreach to [WHO YOU SELL TO], so I care about changes
that alter their budget, their risk, or their day to day work.

Return exactly this:

WHAT CHANGED
Three to five items. Each one gets: the change in one sentence, the date, a
source link, and one line on why a [BUYER TITLE] would care.

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE
Two things people think changed but did not. This is where most outreach
embarrasses itself.

THE OPENER LINE
Three specific observations I could open a message with. Each must be true,
checkable, and NOT something the prospect would read as a press release.

Rules:
- Every claim gets a source link. No link, cut the claim.
- Nothing older than 30 days unless you flag it as background.
- No vendor marketing copy. If the only source is the vendor, say so.

## Watch for

anything without a link. Cut it before it reaches a message.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
