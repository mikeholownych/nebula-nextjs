# Industry Pulse

**Job:** Signal Hunter  
**Profile:** `market`  
**Purpose:** a weekly readout on one vertical. Who raised, who cut, who shipped, who got acquired, and which of those is a reason to reach out.

## Prompt template

Write my weekly pulse on [INDUSTRY OR VERTICAL].

Cover the last 7 days only. Structure it exactly like this:

MONEY
Who raised, who was acquired, who cut headcount. Company, amount or size,
date, source.

MOVEMENT
Who changed a leader in a role I sell to. New name, old name, the role, date.

SHIPPED
What products or major features launched that touch my buyer's workflow.

THE THREE I WOULD TOUCH THIS WEEK
Pick three from everything above. For each: the company, the trigger, the
person to reach, and the one sentence I would open with.

Rules:
- Seven days. Not thirty.
- If a week is quiet, say it is quiet. Do not pad.
- Sources on everything in MONEY and MOVEMENT.

## Watch for

quiet weeks. A padded pulse is worse than a short one, because you will act on filler.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
