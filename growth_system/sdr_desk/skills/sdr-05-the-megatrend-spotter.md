# The Megatrend Spotter

**Job:** Signal Hunter  
**Profile:** `market`  
**Purpose:** looks past this week and flags the shift your buyer will be budgeting for in two quarters, so you get there before the category is crowded.

## Prompt template

I sell [WHAT YOU SELL] to [WHO YOU SELL TO].

Ignore this week. Tell me what my buyer will be budgeting for in 2 to 4
quarters, and why.

Return:

THE SHIFT
Two or three shifts. Each one: what is moving, the evidence it is already
starting, and roughly when it hits a budget line.

THE EVIDENCE
For each shift, the leading indicators you are reading. Hiring patterns,
funding flows, regulation, what leaders repeat on earnings calls, what is
being deprecated.

WHY IT IS NOT OBVIOUS YET
For each shift, why most people in my market have not priced it in.

THE POSITION
If I wanted to be the obvious choice when that budget opens, what would I
need to be publicly saying now.

Rules:
- No predictions without a present tense indicator you can point at.
- If a shift is already crowded, say so and move on.

## Watch for

confident futurism with nothing underneath it. Every shift needs something happening today.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
