# The ICP Magnet

**Job:** ICP Scorer  
**Profile:** `market`  
**Purpose:** works backwards from your best customers and writes the profile you should have been targeting all along.

## Prompt template

Work backwards from my best customers and write the ICP I should be using.

MY BEST 5 CUSTOMERS, by revenue, retention and how easy they were to serve:
[PASTE: who they are, what they do, how they found me, what problem they had
the week they bought, who signed, how long the deal took]

Return:

THE PATTERN
What these five genuinely share. Not industry and headcount, which is lazy.
Look for the SITUATION they were in the week they decided.

THE TRIGGER
What had just happened to each of them. If four of five had the same thing
happen, that is my whole targeting strategy, say so loudly.

THE PROFILE
Written as a paragraph I could hand a researcher, not a bullet list of
firmographics. It should describe a moment, not a company type.

WHO TO STOP CHASING
The profile I am probably wasting time on, based on what is absent here.

Rules:
- Situation beats firmographics. Lead with situation.
- If the five have nothing real in common, say so. That is a finding.

## Watch for

it reaching for headcount and industry because those are easy. Push it back to the situation.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
