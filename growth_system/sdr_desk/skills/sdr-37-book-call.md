# Book Call

**Job:** Meeting Qualifier  
**Profile:** `support`  
**Purpose:** sends the booking message, handles the reschedule, and locks the slot without four emails of ping pong.

## Prompt template

Get this booked in as few messages as possible.

THE CONVERSATION SO FAR: [PASTE]
MY AVAILABILITY: [PASTE REAL SLOTS OR MY BOOKING LINK]
MEETING LENGTH: [MINUTES]
MY VOICE PROFILE: [PASTE]

Write:

THE BOOKING MESSAGE
Under 40 words. Offer two specific times AND the link. Two named times beat a
bare link, because a link asks them to do work.

THE RESCHEDULE
For when they cannot make either. Warm, no friction, no guilt.

THE CONFIRMATION
Sent once booked. Includes what we will cover in three bullets and one question
for them to think about. This is what stops the no show.

THE DAY BEFORE
One line. Confirm and give them an easy out. An easy out reduces no shows, it
does not cause them.

Rules:
- Never send more than two time options. Three creates a decision.
- Never write "let me know what works". That puts the work on them.

## Watch for

the bare calendar link. Two named times converts better every time.

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
