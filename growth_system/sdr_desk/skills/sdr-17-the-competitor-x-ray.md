# The Competitor X-Ray

**Job:** Prospect Researcher  
**Profile:** `market`  
**Purpose:** works out who they are probably using today and what that vendor is bad at, so you have something to say.

## Prompt template

Work out what [COMPANY] is probably using today for [CATEGORY], and where it
hurts.

Evidence to check: their job postings, their tech listed publicly, integration
pages, case studies they appear in, what their team mentions in public posts,
their careers page requirements.

Return:

MOST LIKELY STACK
What they are probably on, with your confidence and the evidence behind it.

WHERE THAT VENDOR IS WEAK
The real, documented weaknesses of that vendor for a company of this shape.
Use public reviews and complaints, not my marketing claims.

THE SWITCHING COST
Honestly. What would be painful about moving. If it is high, my message needs
to be about coexisting, not replacing, and you should tell me that.

THE ANGLE
One sentence that acknowledges what they have and opens a door, without
trashing their current vendor.

Rules:
- Never write an attack line. Buyers defend the choices they made.
- If the evidence is weak, say GUESS and say what would confirm it.

## Watch for

trash talk. It loses deals. The angle should respect the choice they already made.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
