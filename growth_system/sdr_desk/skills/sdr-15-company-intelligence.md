# Company Intelligence

**Job:** Prospect Researcher  
**Profile:** `market`  
**Purpose:** one page on the company. What they sell, who they compete with, what they just changed, and where you fit.

## Prompt template

One page on [COMPANY], written for someone about to send them a cold message.

Structure exactly this, in prose:

WHAT THEY ACTUALLY SELL
In plain words, not their marketing language. Who pays them and for what.

WHO THEY COMPETE WITH
Named. And one line on how they position against them.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE LAST 90 DAYS
Funding, leadership, product, pricing, headcount, market. Dated, with sources.

WHERE THEY ARE INVESTING
Read it off their job postings. Hiring is the most honest strategy document a
company publishes.

WHERE I FIT
Given I sell [WHAT], the one specific place my thing touches their situation.
If there is no good fit, say NO FIT and explain. I would rather know.

THE OPENING LINE
One line, referencing something specific from above, that could not be sent to
any other company.

Rules:
- Their careers page beats their about page. Always.
- If the opening line would work for a competitor of theirs, it is too generic.
  Rewrite it.

## Watch for

an opening line that is portable. If it fits any company in the sector, it fits none of them.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
