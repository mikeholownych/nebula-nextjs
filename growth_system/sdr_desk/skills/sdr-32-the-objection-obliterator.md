# The Objection Obliterator

**Job:** Reply Classifier  
**Profile:** `support`  
**Purpose:** takes the objection you just got and writes three responses at different levels of directness.

## Prompt template

Handle this objection.

THE OBJECTION, their exact words: [PASTE]
WHO THEY ARE: [ROLE, COMPANY]
WHERE WE ARE: [FIRST REPLY, MID CONVERSATION, POST DEMO]
MY VOICE PROFILE: [PASTE]

First, tell me what the objection ACTUALLY is. Stated objections are usually
not the real one. Name both and say which you are answering.

Then three responses:
1. THE QUESTION. Answer with a question that finds out what they really mean.
   Usually the right move.
2. THE REFRAME. Accept the objection as true and change what it is being
   measured against.
3. THE DIRECT. Answer it flatly and ask for the next step.

Each under 70 words.

Then: which one, and why, given where we are.

Rules:
- Never argue that they are wrong. Nobody has ever been argued into a purchase.
- If the honest answer is that they are right and I am not a fit, say that and
  write the graceful exit instead.

## Watch for

it defending you when the honest answer is that you are not a fit.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
