# Communication Blueprints

**Job:** Voice Writer  
**Profile:** `growth`  
**Purpose:** templates for the awkward messages. The breakup, the re-engage, the wrong person handoff, the apology.

## Prompt template

Write me the awkward message set, in my voice.

MY VOICE PROFILE: [PASTE]
CONTEXT: I sell [WHAT] to [WHO].

Write one of each. Keep every one under 80 words.

1. THE BREAKUP. They went quiet after real interest. No guilt, no passive
   aggression, and it must leave the door open.
2. THE RE-ENGAGE. Six months cold. Something has changed since. Lead with the
   change, not with "just checking in".
3. THE WRONG PERSON. They told me it is not them. Ask for the handoff in a way
   that makes it easy to say a name.
4. THE APOLOGY. I got something wrong about their business. Short, no grovelling.
5. THE PRICE PUSHBACK. They said too expensive. Do not discount, do not defend,
   ask the question that finds out what they mean.
6. THE NO SHOW. They missed a call. Warm, zero guilt, one click to rebook.

Rules:
- No "just following up". No "bumping this to the top of your inbox". If you
  use either, start again.
- Every one ends with one clear, small ask.

## Watch for

politeness padding. Under eighty words is the discipline.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
