# The Network Nurturer

**Job:** Reply Classifier  
**Profile:** `support`  
**Purpose:** keeps the people who said "not now" genuinely warm with something useful, so the later date is not wasted.

## Prompt template

Keep my "not now" pile warm without pestering them.

THE PILE:
[PASTE: name, company, what they said, the date they named, when we last spoke]

For each person return:
| Who | Their stated timing | Touch date | What I send | Why it is useful to THEM |

The "what I send" must be genuinely useful standing alone. Something they would
be glad to receive from a friend. Not a case study, not a product update, not a
newsletter.

Then:
THE CALENDAR. Everything above sorted by date so I can see my next 90 days.
THE ONES TO DROP. Anyone whose "not now" was really a polite no. Say how you
can tell, and stop spending on them.

Rules:
- One touch per person per 6 weeks maximum. More than that is pestering.
- Never send a "just checking in". If there is nothing useful to send, the
  answer is send nothing and wait.

## Watch for

the drop list. Some "not now" answers are polite noes, and chasing them is how you waste a quarter.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
