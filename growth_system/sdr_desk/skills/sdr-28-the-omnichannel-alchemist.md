# The Omnichannel Alchemist

**Job:** Sequence Builder  
**Profile:** `growth`  
**Purpose:** takes one message and reshapes it for the inbox, the DM, and the comment section, without it reading like the same thing three times.

## Prompt template

Reshape this one message for three places.

MY VOICE PROFILE: [PASTE]
THE CORE MESSAGE: [PASTE]
THE PERSON: [WHO]

Return three versions:

EMAIL
Subject plus body. Under 90 words. Can carry the most context.

DIRECT MESSAGE
Under 40 words. No subject. Assumes less patience and more informality.

PUBLIC COMMENT
Under 30 words. Adds value to THEIR post with no pitch at all. If a pitch
appears here, you have failed the skill.

Then: THE ORDER. Which one goes first, how long between them, and what makes
me skip straight to the next.

Rules:
- These must not read as three copies of one thing. Different opening, different
  angle, same underlying point.
- The comment version must be genuinely useful standing alone, to someone who
  never clicks my profile.

## Watch for

a pitch in the comment. It reads as a pitch to everyone, and it costs you the account.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
