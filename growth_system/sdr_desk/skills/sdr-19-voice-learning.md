# Voice Learning

**Job:** Voice Writer  
**Profile:** `growth`  
**Purpose:** feed it a handful of things you have actually written and it extracts your rhythm, so everything downstream sounds like you.

## Prompt template

Learn how I write. Do not write anything yet.

HERE ARE THINGS I ACTUALLY WROTE:
[PASTE 5 TO 10 REAL SAMPLES. Emails, messages, posts. Include at least two
you dashed off quickly, they are the most honest.]

Return a VOICE PROFILE:

SENTENCE SHAPE
Average length. Do I run sentences together or clip them. Where do I break.

VOCABULARY
Words I reach for. Words I clearly avoid. My level of formality.

STRUCTURE
How I open. How I close. Do I ask questions. Do I use lists.

TICS
The things I do repeatedly that make it sound like me. Include the slightly
awkward ones. Those matter most.

WHAT I NEVER DO
Constructions absent from all my samples. This list stops you sounding generic.

THE TEST
Write two versions of the same short message. One in my voice, one in
standard business voice, so I can see you have actually got it.

Rules:
- Do not clean me up. If I start sentences with And, keep it.
- Save this profile. Every writing skill after this one refers back to it.

## Watch for

it improving you. Improved is not you, and the whole point is you.
