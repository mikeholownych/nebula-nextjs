# The Bottleneck Detective

**Job:** Meeting Qualifier  
**Profile:** `support`  
**Purpose:** looks across the whole desk and names the single stage costing you the most, so you fix one thing instead of tinkering with five.

## Prompt template

Find my single biggest bottleneck. One. Not a list.

THE FUNNEL, last [PERIOD]:
Contacts reached: [N]
Replies: [N]
Positive replies: [N]
Meetings booked: [N]
Meetings held: [N]
Opportunities: [N]
Closed won: [N]

BENCHMARKS I BELIEVE, if I have any: [PASTE OR WRITE NONE]

Return:

THE CONVERSION MAP
Every stage to stage rate, as a percentage.

THE BOTTLENECK
The ONE stage losing the most. Not the lowest percentage, the stage where
fixing it adds the most closed won. Show the arithmetic.

WHAT IT WOULD BE WORTH
If that one stage improved by a realistic amount, what happens to closed won.
Show the maths so I can argue with it.

WHY IT IS PROBABLY HAPPENING
Three likely causes, ranked, each with how I would check it this week.

WHAT TO IGNORE
The stages I should leave alone even though they look bad. Fixing the wrong
stage is how quarters get wasted.

Rules:
- One bottleneck. If you name two, you have not done the arithmetic.
- Show the working. I want to be able to disagree with your maths.

## Watch for

a list of problems. The value here is the refusal to give you more than one.

The order to build them in
Do not install all forty two on day one. You will use nine of them and forget the rest.

Week one, build Job 3 and Job 4. Research and voice. Those two alone change your reply rate.

Week two, add Job 6. Sorting replies is where the leak is on almost every desk.

Week three, add Job 1 and Job 2, so you stop working a stale list.

Week four, add Job 5 and Job 7 and let the whole thing run.

What to never hand over
Three things stay with you, permanently.

The decision to contact someone. The desk suggests. You approve. The day that flips is the day you become the thing everyone complains about.

The price conversation. Let it draft. Never let it send.

A bad reply. When someone is annoyed, a human answers. Every time.

Everything else on this list is fair game, and every hour it takes back is an hour you spend on the calls that close.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
