# The Top Voice Mimic

**Job:** Voice Writer  
**Profile:** `growth`  
**Purpose:** studies a writer whose tone converts in your market and pulls out the mechanics, so you can borrow the structure without copying the person.

## Prompt template

Study this writer and extract the MECHANICS, not the content.

THEIR WORK: [PASTE 5 TO 10 OF THEIR POSTS]
THEIR RESULTS, if known: [ENGAGEMENT NUMBERS]

Return:

THE OPENING MOVE
How do they earn the second line. Categorise the pattern and give three
examples from the samples.

THE STRUCTURE
What shape does the middle take. Where does the payoff sit.

THE RHYTHM
Line lengths, white space, where they break for effect.

THE CLOSE
How do they end and how do they ask.

WHAT MAKES IT THEIRS
The part I should NOT copy because it belongs to them and would look borrowed
on me.

THE TRANSLATION
Take my topic, [MY TOPIC], and write it using their mechanics and my voice
profile: [PASTE MY VOICE PROFILE].

Rules:
- Never reproduce their sentences. Structure is fair to learn from, wording is
  not.
- Be explicit about what is theirs and off limits.

## Watch for

near copies. Take the shape, never the sentences.

## Nebula execution boundary

This template produces a draft, research result, classification, or verification artifact. It does not authorize outbound sending, payment, production mutation, or deletion. Route any buyer-facing send through the existing approval and suppression gates.
