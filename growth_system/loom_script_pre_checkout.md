# Nebula Loom Script — Pre-Checkout Delivery
# Usage: Record with screen share on the prospect's audit results page.
# Send this video AFTER the founder replies/engages with the audit.
# The Stripe link appears in the email immediately after this video.

---

## CHUNK 1 (~18 seconds) — Recognition
*[Screen: their audit page, score visible]*

"I'm looking at your page right now.
You scored [X]/10 — and you can see the three signals that are failing.
The thing is, these aren't random issues.
They follow a pattern I've seen on every page that gets traffic but doesn't close."

---

## CHUNK 2 (~20 seconds) — The villain named + specific fix
*[Screen: scroll to the top 3 failing findings]*

"Here's what's actually happening on your page.
[Finding 1 label] — [one sentence in visitor voice, e.g. 'A stranger lands here and sees a headline about what you do, not what they get.']
[Finding 2 label] — [e.g. 'There's no proof before the ask. Cold traffic doesn't trust claims.']
[Finding 3 label] — [e.g. 'The CTA asks them to act without telling them what changes.']
These three things are why the clicks aren't converting.
Not your ads. Not your audience. These three things."

---

## CHUNK 3 (~15 seconds) — The offer
*[Screen: close on the score card]*

"I fix these three things for $97.
Done in 48 hours.
Same ad spend. Page that closes.
The link is below."

---

## RECORDING NOTES
- Keep screen share on THEIR audit throughout — never your own page.
- Do not read verbatim — use this as a prompt, speak naturally.
- Record multiple takes of each chunk; use #5 from the video pack (best 2-3 seconds per take).
- Total target: under 75 seconds. Do not exceed 90.
- No intro, no "hey guys", no "so" — start speaking on word one.
- Fill in [Finding 1/2/3] from the actual audit before recording.

## CHUNK STITCHING ORDER
1. Chunk 1 (recognition)
2. Chunk 2 (villain + findings — customized per prospect)
3. Chunk 3 (offer — identical every time)

Chunk 3 can be recorded once and reused for all prospects.
Chunks 1-2 need one take per prospect (use their URL and findings).
