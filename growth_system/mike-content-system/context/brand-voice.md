# Mike H brand voice

## Core principles

1. **Evidence before persuasion**: every public claim needs a source, number, observation, or clearly labeled hypothesis.
2. **State the bleed first**: name the failure the buyer feels before explaining the fix.
3. **Compression**: cut warm-up sentences, filler, and duplicate points.
4. **Practitioner voice**: write from work Mike actually did, not from borrowed authority.
5. **One audience, one offer**: never mix AI Syndicate and Nebula in one asset without an explicit campaign brief.
6. **Revenue over activity**: route content toward a measurable buying signal.
7. **Human approval at the edge**: draft freely, publish and send deliberately.

## Sentence rules

- Short sentences at moments of consequence.
- Active voice.
- Use numerals for measured quantities.
- Use plain verbs: found, tested, shipped, broke, fixed, measured.
- No em dashes.
- No fake certainty, invented urgency, or unsupported outcomes.
- No generic opener such as "In today's fast-paced world."
- No "game changer," "unlock," "leverage," "transformative," "seamless," or "revolutionary."
- No motivational filler or reassurance loops.
- No emoji decoration in serious posts.

## Mike sounds like

- "The ad is not always the leak."
- "That is a symptom, not a diagnosis."
- "Before changing the audience, show me the page."
- "I built the tool. Then it exposed my own page."
- "The number is useful only if we can explain what it measures."

## Mike does not sound like

- "I'm thrilled to announce an exciting new paradigm."
- "This game-changing system unlocks exponential growth."
- "Here are 5 simple hacks to crush your goals."
- "You are absolutely right. Great question."

## Public content quality gate

- Does the first line name a concrete pain, observation, or claim?
- Is the audience unambiguous?
- Is the evidence real, current, and scoped to a denominator?
- Does the body explain the mechanism, not just the finding?
- Does it teach a principle rather than copy a third-party post?
- Is there one useful next step?
- Is the CTA native to the platform and appropriate to the funnel?
- Would Mike say this out loud without sounding like a marketing department?
- Are all numbers, URLs, people, and outcomes safe to publish?
