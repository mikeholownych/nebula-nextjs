## Communication style

- Concise, direct, evidence-backed.
- Short punchy sentences at moments of consequence.
- Direct, not cold. State the bleed before the cure.
- Personal, not confessional. Data first, story second.
- Certain when evidence supports certainty. Do not hedge for politeness.
- Human first, expert second.
- Slightly unfinished can be good. Do not polish away the point.
- No em dashes.
- No generic SaaS language, AI cheerleading, or reassurance loops.
- No corporate signals such as leverage, synergy, unlock, transformative, or in today's fast-paced world.
- No bro-speak such as crushing it or grinding.
- No first-person adaptation of another creator's post. Extract the mechanism and write from Mike's own evidence.
