# Mike H hook library

Source: normalized from the supplied viral_hooks.md. These are patterns, not permission to invent proof.

## Result and transformation

- "I [verified result] after [specific action]. Here is what changed."
- "This went from [before state] to [after state]. The fix was [mechanism]."
- "I turned [ordinary input] into [useful output] without [common waste]."

## Problem and enemy

- "The reason [audience] cannot [desired result] is not [wrong diagnosis]. It is [observed mechanism]."
- "Everyone tells you to [common advice]. That misses [specific condition]."
- "Your [asset] is not broken because of [popular explanation]. Look at [evidence] first."

## Confession and story

- "I built [thing] while missing [obvious problem]."
- "I spent [time or money] on [wrong move] before checking [evidence]."
- "I was doing the work. They were telling the story. Then the numbers caught up."
- "I thought [belief]. The audit showed [contradiction]."

## If-then and diagnosis

- "If you are [trigger state] and want [result], check [specific signal] before [common action]."
- "If your ads get clicks but the page gets no action, start here."
- "If you cannot explain what the visitor should do in the first viewport, do not buy more traffic yet."

## Comparison and myth busting

- "This page says [category]. That page says [outcome]. The visitor has to choose quickly."
- "More traffic versus clearer message. One of these is easier to measure."
- "A visible CTA is not the same as a clear next step."
- "A score is not a diagnosis. Here is the signal behind it."

## Authority and proof

- "I audited [verified count] pages. The recurring failure was [specific finding]."
- "[Verified number]. Here is what that number actually measures."
- "Before you change [channel], inspect [asset]."
- "The repair is smaller than the redesign people want to sell you."

## Hook rules

- Make the first line specific.
- Use a real number only when its source and denominator are known.
- Do not use a borrowed creator's story as Mike's story.
- Do not use "viral," "secret," "hack," or "guaranteed" without evidence.
- The hook must create a question the body can answer.
- Prefer a concrete symptom over a broad aspiration.
