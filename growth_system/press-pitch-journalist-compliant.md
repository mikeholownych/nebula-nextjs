## What Changed (vs original draft)

### Rule 2 — Personalization
**Added**: `I noticed you cover [specific piece] — this fits directly.`
Before sending to each journalist, replace with a specific article they wrote. This is the #1 rejection reason (8/10 rejections = not relevant to their beat).

### Rule 4 — Multimedia
**Added**: Attachments line and methodology note.
Before each send, attach:
- `scorecard-example.png` — sample audit output
- `grade-distribution-chart.png` — 40B/43C/3D/0A visual

**DO NOT** write "let me know if you want images." 53% of journalists reject that.

### Rule 7 — VIP/Exclusive
**Added**: "Happy to offer first access before the public release on September 2."
This converts a broadcast pitch into an exclusive opportunity.
- Offer Tier A journalists (MarTech, SEJ) a 48h embargo window before Sep 2
- Tier B gets the story on Sep 2 simultaneously

### Rule 8 — Not a sales pitch
**Changed**: "Launching on Product Hunt September 2" → "public release on September 2"
PH launch is the backstop, not the lede. The story is the data, not the product launch.
