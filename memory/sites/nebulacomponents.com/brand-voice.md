# Nebula Components — Brand Voice

Tone: clinical, direct, evidence-first. Building inspector, not warm agency.

## Positioning

Nebula is the evidence standard for conversion. Before changing the ad, audience, or offer, run the audit.

## Vocabulary

- finding, not issue
- signal, not metric
- defect, not opportunity
- One-Leak Repair Sprint, not fix pack
- score is the internal 0–100 value; grade is the public A–F display

## Buying Trigger

Founders actively spending on paid traffic while the landing page produces weak or zero conversions.

## Offer

1. Free audit — 9 signals, under 2 minutes, no email required to see results.
2. $97 One-Leak Repair Sprint — targeted prompts for one highest-confidence failing signal.
3. No retainer-first framing before verified customer outcomes.

## Claims Discipline

- Name the observable condition and attach evidence.
- Do not promise conversion lift or revenue outcomes without measured proof.
- Do not describe Nebula as a CRO agency, landing page builder, or generic SEO tool.
