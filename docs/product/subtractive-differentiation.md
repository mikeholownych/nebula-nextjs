# Subtractive differentiation (standing doctrine)

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: binding until the observatory gate is inspected.
Gate: 500 stamped production audits AND 14 days after 2026-08-31 22:00 UTC.
P1 finding defensibility is frozen. T0 marketing is frozen. Scoring is frozen.

Thesis: Nebula is not trying to know more things about a webpage than everyone else. It is trying to make fewer claims that are substantially harder to dispute.

Same-condition re-observation proves the diagnosed condition changed. It does not prove conversion impact. That boundary is differentiation, not a defect to hide.

---

## Refuse (do not compete, do not build from competitor research)

- More checks
- More AI recommendations
- SEO breadth (backlinks, keyword ideas, Ahrefs-lite dashboards)
- Cheap scan SKUs
- Modeled revenue impact
- Generic CRO heuristics
- Opaque scoring
- Public reliability percentages invented before production evidence

Adopt-nothing from a competitor review is a successful outcome.

## Own now (already in product / P1)

- Paid-traffic-specific diagnosis (buying trigger, not a demographic)
- Finding-level evidence
- Explicit uncertainty (INDETERMINATE, NOT_APPLICABLE, not_established)
- Same-condition verification (same condition_id + version, FAIL to PASS is a condition change only)

## Develop later (not now)

- Challengeable determinations with a recorded outcome
- Capture-integrity transparency as a customer-facing reliability story

Public rates such as "98.7% determinable" are forbidden until the observatory has a real denominator and closed-set definitions.

---

## Ranked "neither can" list (conceptual, not a sprint)

1. Same-condition re-observation tied to the original conversion-context diagnosis, without claiming conversion impact. Strongest commercially. P1 primitive exists. Observe.
2. Challengeable determinations. Strongest structurally. Do not build during the freeze.
3. Paid-traffic-context as an issuance constraint, not just copy. Strongest positioning. Do not expand scoring to invent this.
4. Transparent diagnostic reliability. Strongest credibility. Instrumentation first. Publishing a raw failure percentage is optional later, not the capability itself.

---

## How to use this in a competitor review

Ask only:

1. What can they do that Nebula cannot?
2. What can neither do that a customer would materially value?

If the answer to (1) is on the Refuse list, stop. Do not create tickets.
If the answer to (2) is already owned or frozen under observation, stop.
