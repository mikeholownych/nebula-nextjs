# Expanded Nebula Hypothesis Design

**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Status:** Approved

## Objective

Expand Nebula's foundational hypothesis without broadening its current product
promise. Nebula will continue to sell landing-page diagnosis and bounded repair
while researching the larger problem of how founders identify constraints in
their growth systems.

## Strategic decision

Nebula will distinguish between its worldview and its current commercial wedge:

- **Worldview:** Founders lack a reliable methodology for identifying the
  constraint currently limiting growth.
- **Current product:** Nebula diagnoses observable landing-page friction and
  repairs one high-confidence, page-level constraint.
- **Future possibility:** Nebula may add other diagnostic domains after the
  research demonstrates that it can observe, diagnose, and verify them
  reliably.

The broader hypothesis will guide research, content, and future product
development. The narrower hypothesis will govern what Nebula currently claims,
sells, and delivers.

## Hypothesis architecture

### Foundational hypothesis

> Founders lack a reliable methodology for identifying the constraint currently
> limiting growth. Consequently, they often optimize visible symptoms rather
> than the part of the system governing throughput.

### Current product hypothesis

> For founders already acquiring traffic, the landing page may be the active
> constraint. Nebula can diagnose observable landing-page friction, identify a
> bounded repair, and verify that the repair was implemented.

Nebula must not claim that the landing page is always the constraint. The
landing page is Nebula's first diagnostic domain, not its final product
boundary.

The hierarchy is:

```text
Growth constraint methodology
└── Acquisition-to-conversion diagnosis
    └── Landing-page constraint detection
        └── One-Leak Repair Sprint
```

## Scope boundary

This design changes Nebula's strategic hypothesis and research direction. It
does not expand the present audit or repair-sprint product contract.

The current product remains bounded to:

- observable landing-page conditions;
- high-confidence page-level findings;
- one approved, bounded repair;
- production verification and same-scope re-audit; and
- outcome claims only when supported by adequate outcome data.

Nebula will not present itself as a whole-business growth diagnostician until it
has evidence and capabilities that justify that promise.

## Research objective

The first research cycle will determine how founders currently diagnose growth
constraints and where that process breaks.

The primary research question is:

> When growth underperforms, how do founders decide what to fix next-and what
> causes them to choose the wrong constraint?

The current ICP will be used as an initial recruitment channel, not treated as a
validated assumption. Re-evaluating the ICP is explicitly deferred until the
first constraint-research cycle produces evidence.

## Research sequence

Nebula will use a staged hybrid approach:

1. Conduct 10–15 open-ended founder interviews.
2. Derive a provisional constraint and diagnostic-failure taxonomy from
   recurring patterns.
3. Add a lightweight growth-system snapshot around the existing audit flow.
4. Select 3–5 cases for longitudinal observation as founders diagnose, act, and
   measure results.
5. Re-evaluate the ICP using the resulting evidence.

This ordering prevents Nebula from encoding an untested constraint taxonomy into
the audit questionnaire.

## Interview design

Each interview will reconstruct one real growth decision rather than solicit
general opinions.

The interviewer will establish:

1. What outcome was underperforming?
2. What evidence did the founder examine?
3. Which possible constraints did the founder consider?
4. What did the founder decide to change?
5. Why did that explanation win?
6. What happened after the change?
7. What evidence would cause the founder to revise the diagnosis?

For each case, Nebula will capture:

- business stage and growth motion;
- desired outcome and observed performance;
- suspected constraint;
- diagnostic process used;
- evidence available, used, and ignored;
- action taken;
- time and money committed;
- measured result;
- whether the diagnosis changed afterward; and
- confidence before and after the intervention.

The first interview round is discovery, not a sales exercise. Nebula will not
pitch a whole-system product during these interviews. Unprompted expressions of
demand may be recorded as secondary evidence but will not replace direct
willingness-to-pay research later.

## Provisional analysis outputs

Interview analysis will produce:

- a map of how founders decide what to optimize;
- a provisional taxonomy of constraint types;
- a provisional taxonomy of diagnostic failure modes;
- the evidence founders use and omit;
- patterns in misdiagnosis, intervention selection, and belief revision; and
- questions suitable for structured measurement in the audit flow.

Possible failure modes include optimizing what is easiest to change, accepting
channel-specific explanations without system evidence, confusing correlation
with causation, and lacking visibility between funnel stages. These are
sensitizing examples only; they are not predetermined research findings.

## Positioning

Nebula will continue to lead publicly with the concrete landing-page problem.
The broader growth-system thesis will appear beneath the offer as its
intellectual foundation.

The positioning principle is:

> A landing page is one component of a growth system. Nebula determines whether
> observable page friction is constraining the path from traffic to action,
> without pretending the page explains every growth problem.

The public offer should communicate:

> Identify and repair the highest-confidence constraint visible on your landing
> page.

All messaging and product outputs must preserve these distinctions:

- The audit identifies page-level constraints, not the company's universal
  growth constraint.
- Findings distinguish observations, symptoms, suspected constraints, and
  demonstrated constraints.
- The repair sprint resolves one bounded page-level issue.
- Nebula does not claim conversion lift without adequate outcome data.
- When evidence points beyond the page, Nebula says so explicitly.

## Audit research bridge

After the interviews establish a provisional taxonomy, Nebula may add a
lightweight growth snapshot to the audit journey. It should gather:

- the founder's current growth objective;
- the funnel stage believed to be underperforming;
- evidence supporting that belief;
- other interventions attempted;
- the result of the most recent intervention; and
- confidence in the current diagnosis.

The snapshot is a research instrument. It must not imply that the current audit
can diagnose the entire growth system.

## Expansion gates

Nebula will add a diagnostic domain only when all three conditions are met:

1. A recurring diagnostic failure appears across multiple founders.
2. Nebula can observe enough of the relevant system to diagnose it reliably.
3. Nebula can recommend or perform a bounded intervention and verify the
   result.

Domains will be added one at a time. Nebula will not jump directly to a generic
AI growth-advisor product.

A tentative learning sequence is:

```text
Landing page
→ Traffic-to-page message match
→ Activation or checkout
→ Offer and pricing
→ Acquisition efficiency
→ Retention
```

This sequence is a research hypothesis, not a product roadmap. Evidence may
change the order or exclude domains entirely.

## First-cycle success criteria

The first research cycle succeeds when Nebula can state, with traceable
interview and audit evidence:

- how founders currently choose what to fix;
- the recurring ways those decisions fail;
- which failure modes occur in the existing audit audience;
- whether landing pages are commonly diagnosed with evidence or merely blamed;
  and
- which adjacent constraint, if any, is suitable for Nebula's next diagnostic
  domain.

## Deferred ICP decision

ICP re-evaluation begins only after the first research cycle. It will compare
founder segments using:

- recurrence and urgency of the constraint;
- observability of the relevant system;
- Nebula's ability to intervene;
- verifiability of the outcome; and
- willingness to pay.

Until then, the current ICP is a practical source of participants and customers,
not a settled definition of the future market.
