## Recurring buying triggers

Patterns that appear across adjacent category copy **and** G2 positioning (recurring, not one-off):

1. **Paid traffic with weak conversion** (Unbounce, LandingBoost, Fibr ad-personalization). OBSERVED positioning.
2. **Need to ship pages without engineering** (Unbounce G2/product: marketers building without devs). OBSERVED.
3. **Need to see why users drop** (Hotjar Observe/Ask). OBSERVED.
4. **Need stakeholder-proof for design choices** (Attention Insight "defend with data"). OBSERVED.
5. **Need a second opinion before ads** (LandingBoost, Cruelx, Nebula). OBSERVED.

Nebula ICP (founders bleeding ad spend with zero conversions) matches (1) and (5), not (2). Unbounce wins (2). Do not become a builder.

## Competitor weaknesses usable as vectors

**Structural (multiple independent observations):**

1. **DIY remainder.** LandingBoost, Cruelx, LandingScore, PageAudit all stop at advice.
2. **Traffic-dependent tools.** Hotjar/VWO need volume. Founders with zero conversions cannot use them. That is Nebula's beachhead.
3. **Builder lock-in.** Unbounce meters hosted visitors.
4. **Sales tax.** Fibr/VWO/Unbounce concierge require a meeting.
5. **Unsupported lift claims.** LandingScore 40%+, Fibr $50M+, various "AI-powered" pages. Buyers who have been burned will punish this. Nebula must not join.

**Not yet a pattern (one-off / unverified):**

- LandingBoost PeerPush 21 reviews: too small, possibly friendly network. SPECULATIVE.
- Anonymous Cruelx testimonials. Not a pattern until sourced.
