# Nebula Agency Partner Pilot Protocol

**Status:** Design only, not launched
**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Decision owner:** Mike Holownych

## Purpose

Test whether one agency partner can use Nebula's existing audit and One-Leak Repair Sprint workflow to create qualified client opportunities. This protocol is a bounded validation experiment. It is not a broad agency program, a recurring pricing launch, or proof of agency demand.

## Pilot package

This is one pilot package, not a broad agency program. There is no broad agency program launch in this protocol. It has:

- one agency partner;
- up to three explicitly consented client landing pages;
- one public-page audit receipt per client;
- one bounded repair artifact when a client purchases the `$97` One-Leak Repair Sprint;
- one same-scope re-audit within 30 days; and
- one referral source tag linking the opportunity to the partner.

The package uses existing verified capabilities only. It does not promise a dashboard, multi-domain workspace, automated white-label delivery, client-side implementation, conversion lift, revenue improvement, or recurring agency service.

## Referral path

Use one referral path only:

`agency partner link → Nebula public audit → audit receipt → client-approved repair sprint → attributable Stripe payment`

The referral URL must carry a stable source tag. Qualified discovery, audit starts, replies, clicks, and booked conversations are diagnostic signals. An attributable payment is the success signal.

## Data and consent boundary

Require client consent before processing client audit data. The consent record must identify the client, permitted data scope, timestamp, and agency relationship. Require separate publication permission before publishing client audit data, screenshots, logos, names, testimonials, or outcomes.

No client data may be used for a public comparison, case study, or training artifact without the required publication permission. Internal pilot records may use pseudonymous identifiers, but the payment receipt and audit receipt must remain traceable to authorized internal records.

## White-label boundary

White-label claims must remain limited to capabilities verified in the running product. The pilot may provide a factual referral handoff and the existing audit result. It must not represent the current product as an automated white-label platform unless that capability is separately verified and approved.

Do not publish partner logos, client logos, testimonials, conversion percentages, revenue outcomes, or superiority claims without permission and primary evidence. Do not promise an outcome that the current product does not measure.

## Stop rules

Stop the pilot when any of the following occurs:

- three qualified partner conversations produce no attributable opportunity;
- the fixed pilot window ends without a qualified opportunity;
- the partner requests unsupported claims or unverified capabilities;
- consent or attribution cannot be recorded;
- processing client data exceeds the agreed scope; or
- the pilot creates a legal, privacy, or brand risk.

A pilot may proceed to review only when its records show the referral source, audit receipt, repair artifact where applicable, consent boundary, and payment status.

## Review decision

At the end of the pilot window, classify the result as `OPEN_VALIDATION`, `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, or `STOPPED`. A positive reply, audit, or checkout click cannot move the pilot to a revenue-success conclusion. Any expansion requires a separate decision based on qualified partner opportunities or attributable payments.
