# Nebula backlink acquisition register

Date: 2026-09-06
Owner: CEO / Growth
Objective: increase qualified discovery for founders spending on paid traffic without conversions.

## Operating rule

Backlinks are an acquisition input, not a revenue claim. Every acquired link must be measured through:

`referring page -> qualified visit -> audit start -> audit delivery -> attributable payment`

No paid-link schemes, link farms, automated mass submissions, fabricated reviews, or irrelevant guest posts.

## Acquisition sequence

1. Verify existing public listings and correct canonical URL usage.
2. Validate Tier 1 editorial targets and their actual submission/contact policies.
3. Prepare one factual listing packet: short description, canonical URL, screenshots, methodology, and evidence boundaries.
4. Submit or contact only the validated Tier 1 targets.
5. Add UTM/referrer tracking and record every outcome.
6. Review qualified sessions and audit starts after each placement.
7. Expand to Tier 2 only when Tier 1 produces signal or a clear strategic rationale.

- Tracking: source, medium, campaign, referring page, qualified session, audit start, payment

## Kill criteria

Stop or revise a backlink lane when:

- the target is irrelevant or primarily sells links;
- the listing cannot use the canonical domain safely;
- traffic arrives without qualified visits after a defined test window;
- the source requires unsupported claims;
- the placement creates brand or compliance risk;
- three comparable placements produce no qualified discovery signal.

## Machine-readable placement contract

Each placement record must conform to `backlink-placement.schema.json` and carry:

- status: `candidate`, `contacted`, `accepted`, `live_verified`, `rejected`, or `excluded`;
- target domain and directly verified placement URL;
- canonical destination on `https://nebulacomponents.com`;
- acquisition method and source tag;
- UTM source, medium, and campaign;
- referral sessions, qualified sessions, audit starts, and payments; and
- a three-placement review window with a qualified-session threshold of at least one.

Softrankings is permanently `excluded` from the active queue. Its existing listing is retained only as historical context because it cannot be updated from Mike's side after repeated contact attempts.

No placement record is evidence of a backlink until its live URL and canonical destination are directly verified. A referral session, audit start, or reply is diagnostic; only an attributable payment is a revenue signal.

## Evidence boundary

Search results used for initial target discovery are untrusted external observations. They establish candidate status only. They do not prove authority, traffic, dofollow status, acceptance, or conversion value. Those fields require direct verification from the target source or first-party analytics.
