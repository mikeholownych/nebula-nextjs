## Why this set

The job is not "landing page builders." The job is: turn attention into a diagnosis of conversion leaks, then monetize leftover unresolved conditions.

Three layers:

1. **Direct:** URL-in conversion/landing-page audit products
2. **Adjacent:** builders, experimentation, behavior analytics, free graders that occupy the same buying trigger
3. **Best-in-class:** commercial systems with transferable mechanisms (diagnose-without-resolution, free-tool capture, PLG+sales)

Thin SEO listicles that rank for "best AI landing page audit tools" exist. Several appear to be self-serving comparison pages. They are not treated as category leaders.

**Transferable:** published price ladder, trial, traffic as value metric, professional-services add-on, agency directory. **Not copy:** traffic-cap overages, geo-block ("unavailable in your location"), MCP-as-feature theater.

### VWO (`vwo.com`)

Experimentation. Growth/Pro/Enterprise. Public page is demo-gated; no self-serve dollar amounts on the extracted pricing grid. OBSERVED.

G2 VWO Testing: 4.4/5, 929 reviews. Recurring cons (G2 generated summary): missing features, slow performance, testing difficulties, complexity. OBSERVED as G2 aggregate, not individual quotes.

**Transferable:** guardrail metrics, SRM detection, hypothesis backlog. **Not copy:** sales-gated pricing, "thousands of leading brands" as substitute for a working free diagnostic.

### Hotjar / Contentsquare (`hotjar.com/pricing`)

Behavior analytics. Free plan €0 (200k sessions claimed). Growth €39/yr-billed or €49 list. Pro/Enterprise = let's talk. 30-day money-back on Growth. OBSERVED.

**Transferable:** forever-free qualitative layer, impact quantification on paid, MCP query. **Not copy:** session-cap that stops heatmaps mid-month (FAQ OBSERVED). Needs traffic; useless for a founder with no conversions yet.

### HubSpot Website Grader (`website.grader.com`)

URL **and email required** before score. Free SEO/speed/mobile/security grade. Upsell path is HubSpot CRM/Academy. OBSERVED.

Classic lead-magnet. **DO NOT COPY** email-before-value. Nebula already gives diagnosis without identity. Preserve that.
