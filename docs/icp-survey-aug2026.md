## WHAT THE RESPONSES WILL TELL YOU

If majority answer (a): Active-spend ICP is correct. Messaging stays the same.
Focus: "your ads are live, your page is leaking"

If majority answer (b): Pre-spend ICP hypothesis is right.
Pivot: "Before you spend a dollar on ads, know exactly what your page will do to that traffic"
New positioning: audit as a launch gate, not a fix tool

If majority answer (c): Neither ICP is being served.
Reality: You're attracting curiosity-driven users who have no urgent problem.
This means the traffic source matters more than the ICP definition —
people are coming from "what does my site score" curiosity, not active pain.
The fix is distribution channels closer to the moment of spend decision.
