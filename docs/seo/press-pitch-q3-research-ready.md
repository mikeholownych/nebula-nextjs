## Key Data Points (verified from live DB, 2026-08-28)

- **n = 293** completed landing page audits
- **Headline failures: 62%** of pages — most common single conversion leak
- **Load Speed failures: 40%** of pages (avg impact score: 2.5/5)
- **Social Proof failures: 39%** of pages (avg impact score: 4.1/5 — highest-impact leak)
- **Average conversion leaks per page: 2.7**
- **Social Proof has the highest average impact of any signal (4.1/5)**
