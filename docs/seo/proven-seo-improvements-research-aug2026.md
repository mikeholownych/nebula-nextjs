## 3. ENTITY SEO — 50% better featured snippet inclusion

### Measured evidence
Semrush research: Content recognized as entities in Google's Knowledge Graph
→ **50% more likely to appear in featured snippets**

Google leaked attributes (2024):
- `siteFocusScore`: How tightly the site concentrates on one subject
- `siteRadius`: How far pages stray from core topic
- Sites that go deep on one territory consistently beat generalists
- `contentEffort`: Estimates genuine work in a page; thin rewrites score poorly

### Entity establishment steps (confirmed mechanics)
1. **Wikidata entry** — verifiable entity record that Google's Knowledge Graph indexes
2. **Google Knowledge Panel claim** — signals brand recognition
3. **Consistent NAP** (Name/Address/Person) across all mentions
4. **Wikipedia citation or coverage** — not required but accelerates recognition
5. **Named author with LinkedIn profile** — author entities feed site entity

### Compounding real example
Digital Nomads HQ (SEO agency): 2,500/mo → 35,000+/mo in 24 months
0 → 800+ AI Overview keyword appearances in same window
Strategy: Original research + first-hand data + entity-rich content

## 5. CONTENT FRESHNESS — 60% traffic decay without updates

### Documented decay rates
**Animalz study** (n=60,000 pages):
- Average page loses **60% of peak traffic within 2 years** without updates
- "Evergreen" content loses ~25% over 2 years
- Recovery possible within 12–18 month window from peak

### Refresh ROI vs new content
**Clearscope case** (SaaS company, 150 posts >18 months old):
- Refreshed with updated data + expanded content
- Average traffic lift per refreshed post: **+45%**
- Cost: 2–3 hours per post vs 8–10 hours for new post
- **ROI: ~4× better than new content** for same traffic gain

### What "fresh" means (John Mueller, 2022)
"Updating the date in your content doesn't help if you haven't actually updated the content."
Substantive changes matter. Date-only cosmetic updates do not.

**Query type freshness variance** (Moz analysis):
- News/events queries: Freshness = 40–60% of ranking variance
- How-to/concept queries: Freshness <5% (stable for years)
- Annual data/software/laws: Update within 6–12 months or lose 20–40% of traffic

## 8. CORE WEB VITALS — Baseline requirement, not differentiator

### Google's confirmed stance
- CWV became a ranking factor June 2021 (Page Experience update)
- Google stated directly: poor CWV will not prevent ranking if content is strong; good CWV will not rank weak content
- CWV is a **tiebreaker** between otherwise equal pages, not a multiplier

### Documented business impact (Chrome team research)
- **Vodafone**: 31% improvement in LCP → **8% increase in sales**
- **NDTV**: 55% improvement in LCP → **50% lower bounce rate**
- **Tokopedia**: 14% better LCP → **23% reduction in sessions with bounce**
- Source: Confirmed by Chrome team at web.dev; structured data docs confirm CTR lifts

## What NOT to do

**Buying links:** Google's `siteAuthority` model penalizes link schemes. Every major algo update
since 2012 targets manipulative link patterns. Case: JCPenney (2011) — #1 for all major retail
terms via PBN → manual penalty → disappeared overnight.

**Content volume without depth:** Semrush data shows 20 thin articles ≠ topical authority.
Coverage with substance beats volume without it.

**Technical SEO in isolation:** CWV, schema, structured data are necessary but insufficient.
All three of Rotten Tomatoes, Food Network, and Rakuten already had strong domain authority
before their structured data CTR lifts were measurable. Weak domain = the lift is smaller.

**Publishing without a link acquisition plan:** 94% of new content earns zero backlinks.
Every piece should have a defined outreach target before publication.
