# Nebula Brand OS

Version: 2026-08-15.1
Status: active operating reference

## Positioning

Nebula helps founders spending on ads find the landing-page leaks turning clicks into no conversions, then fix the highest-cost problems for $97.

Rejection line: If you are not sending paid traffic or do not have a live page, this is not the first problem to solve.

## Buyer

- Role: founder or growth owner
- Company: small B2B or service business with a live landing page
- Trigger: paid traffic is producing clicks but no conversions
- Pain: the page looks fine, but the money keeps disappearing
- Offer: free evidence-backed audit, then a $97 One-Leak Repair Sprint

## Content pillars

1. Paid traffic leaks. Show where clicks die after the ad.
2. Evidence-backed diagnosis. Publish measured page findings, not taste opinions.
3. Risk and reputation. Explain how outbound systems classify uncertainty before sending. MailCheck is a risk layer, not a sales-conversion engine.

## Voice

- Short declarative sentences.
- Plain language.
- Name the cost of inaction.
- Blame the page structure, not the founder.
- Use measured evidence whenever available.
- No hype, generic AI language, or unsupported guarantees.
- No em dashes.
- One CTA per asset.

## Proof bank

- 123 completed page audits are recorded in Nebula's audit system.
- The current dual-provider comparison contains 93 contacts.
- 2 contacts were classified CONFIRMED_INVALID and hard-blocked by policy.
- 26 ACCEPT_ALL and 4 unresolved states are uncertainty classifications, not invalidity claims.
- 41 contacts cleared both providers and Nebula policy in the current queue.

## CTA rules

- Primary CTA: run the free audit at https://nebulacomponents.com/audit
- One action per post.
- Use a campaign-tagged URL when publishing.
- Do not promise conversion lift without a measured result.
- Do not frame MailCheck as a sales tool.

## Current experiment

Test whether evidence-backed public teardown content creates qualified audit starts from founders actively buying traffic.

Primary metric: attributable audit starts.
Secondary metrics: completed audits, qualified replies, checkout starts, payments.
Decision rule: keep, adjust, or kill after a bounded batch with attributable data. Impressions alone do not count as traction.
