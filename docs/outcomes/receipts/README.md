## What a receipt is

A **receipt** is a documented before/after for a real engagement: audit → leak found → fix
applied → measured change. Receipts are the proof layer of Nebula's "de facto leader" strategy
(see `docs/leadership-strategy-2026-08-04.md`, Play 2).

- Publishable case studies derive from receipts, never before receipts exist.
- 5–10 confirmed receipts = "leaders sell receipts."
- A receipt without a fix is not a receipt - it's an audit.
- A receipt without a measured before/after is a testimonial - also fine, but not a receipt.

## Attribution standard

### Measurement methods (pick exactly one per receipt)

1. **Score-based (Nebula audit engine)** - the same-scope re-audit or a monitoring run compares
   the 9-signal score before vs after the fix. This is the default for $97 Fix Pack engagements
   (the pricing page already promises a same-scope re-audit within 30 days).
2. **Analytics-based (SOP-005)** - GA4/PostHog/customer analytics compare conversion rate,
   sessions, and bounce before vs after. Required for any claim about conversions.

### Rules (both methods)

1. Baseline must be **before** the deployed changes; minimum 7 days for analytics method.
2. Measurement must start **after** deployment; minimum 7 days for analytics method.
3. No cherry-picking days - use full windows.
4. Document every confounder known at measurement time.
5. Honest conclusion ladder (see schema below) - prefer `improvement_suggested` over
   `improvement_confirmed` unless the evidence is strong.
6. Customer must confirm the outcome before the receipt becomes `confirmed`.
7. Customer must separately consent to publication before a receipt becomes `published`.

### What a score-based receipt may and may not claim

- MAY: "This page moved from failing 6 of 9 conversion signals to passing 8 of 9."
- MAY: "The same-scope re-audit score improved from 4.2 to 6.8 (same URL, same engine, same 9 signals)."
- MAY NOT: "Conversions increased X%." A score change repairs signals; it does not prove
  conversion lift. Only the analytics method (SOP-005) may discuss conversion metrics, and only
  with the >10% / >100-session significance standard.

## Conclusion ladder (score-based receipts)

| Score delta (0–10 scale) | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| `after - before >= +1.0` AND no major unconfounded alternate explanation | `improvement_confirmed` |
| `+0.5 <= after - before < +1.0` | `improvement_suggested` |
| `abs(delta) < 0.5` | `no_change` (within measurement noise) |
| `after - before <= -0.5` | `decline` |
| No baseline or no after (e.g. no monitor/re-audit yet) | `insufficient_data` |

For analytics-based receipts, use SOP-005's ladder exactly
(`improvement_confirmed` = CR > 10% AND > 100 sessions per period).

## Receipt lifecycle

```
draft → (customer confirms outcome) → confirmed → (customer consents to publish) → published
```

- **Draft** - generated by `scripts/receipt_draft_from_monitor.py` (score-based) or hand-written
  (analytics-based). `customer_confirmed = false`, `publishable = false`.
- **Confirmed** - Mike or the engagement owner has the customer's confirmation; set
  `customer_confirmed = true`, `status = confirmed`, `confirmed_at`.
- **Published** - customer consented; `publishable = true`, `status = published`. Now eligible to
  feed `audit_to_case_study.py` / public case studies.
