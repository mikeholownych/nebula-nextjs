## Conversion Gap
**Funnel deficiencies reducing completion/purchase:**

**Observed Funnel from Homepage:**
1. Visit homepage → See free audit offer
2. Enter URL → Get free audit results (no email required)
3. View results → Option to unlock full report with email
4. See highest priority fix → Option to buy $97 Repair Sprint
5. Purchase → Receive exact fix → Implement → Get 30-day re-audit

**Identified Conversion Issues:**
*Due to content delivery issues, cannot verify actual funnel flow, but potential issues include:*
- Lack of social proof/testimonials near audit CTA (observed in homepage teardown examples)
- Unclear value proposition of email unlock (what additional value does full report provide?)
- No live demo or sandbox to try audit without submitting own URL
- Limited explanation of what the 9 signals mean for first-time visitors
- No clear path for agencies or enterprise customers (primarily founder-focused)
