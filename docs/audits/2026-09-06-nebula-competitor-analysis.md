## Competitive Positioning Assessment

Based on Nebula's own documentation and API:

**Nebula's Clear Differentiators:**
1. **Transparent Boundaries:** Explicitly states what the audit can and cannot prove (no conversion lift guarantees)
2. **Evidence-Based Approach:** Each finding tied to actual page evidence with pass/fail criteria
3. **x402 Payment Protocol:** Innovative use of blockchain-based micro-payments for paid audits ($0.10 USDC)
4. **Agent-First Design:** Model Context Protocol tools enabling AI agent integration
5. **Live Dataset Transparency:** Publishes real audit statistics (293+ audits, avg 2.7 leaks/page)
6. **Open Source Core:** Citable open-source evidence layer for verification
7. **Clear ICP Focus:** Founders spending on Google/Meta/LinkedIn ads who aren't converting
8. **Specific Signal Framework:** 9 well-defined conversion signals with documentation
9. **No Black Box:** Rejects AI hype and fake promises in favor of observable diagnostics
10. **Structured Repair Process:** One-Leak Repair Sprint with exact fixes and 30-day re-audit
