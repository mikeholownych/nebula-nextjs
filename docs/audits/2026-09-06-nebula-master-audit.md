# Nebula Components Master Audit Report
**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Auditor:** opencode AI Agent  
**Scope:** Comprehensive audit of nebulacomponents.com as both a public-facing website and landing page audit service

## Executive Finding

Nebula Components operates as a landing page conversion diagnostic service targeting founders spending on paid ads with low conversion rates. The service offers a free automated audit across 9 conversion signals and a paid $97 One-Leak Repair Sprint for targeted fixes.

**What is working:**
- Clear value proposition focused on paid traffic conversion diagnostics
- Transparent service boundaries (no conversion lift guarantees)
- Evidence-based diagnostic approach with observable pass/fail criteria
- Free audit tool provides immediate value without signup
- Structured 9-signal framework with documented standards
- Open-source Citable component for transparency
- Live dataset showing 293+ completed audits

**What is materially broken:**
- Multiple public pages (/about, /pricing, /learning-centre, etc.) return identical documentation content instead of page-specific information
- This suggests a routing or content delivery issue affecting discoverability and user experience
- Prevents users from accessing detailed information about specific services, educational content, and legal pages

**Largest constraint:**
- Content delivery/routing issue preventing access to key informational pages
- Undermines trust, discoverability, and ability to educate potential customers

**Largest opportunity:**
- Fixing content delivery issues to restore access to educational, legal, and service detail pages
- Would improve SEO, user experience, and conversion funnel efficiency

**What should NOT be changed yet:**
- Core 9-signal audit methodology (appears well-defined and evidence-based)
- Free audit -> Repair Sprint business model (clear value exchange)
- Transparent boundaries around what the audit can/cannot prove
