# Nebula Components Conversion Analysis

## Funnel Integrity Assessment

**Observed Free Audit Funnel (from Homepage):**
1. **Landing:** Visit homepage → Clear value proposition and free audit CTA
2. **Engagement:** Enter landing page URL → Submit for free audit
3. **Conversion:** View audit results (score and findings) → Optional email unlock for full report
4. **Monetization:** See highest priority fixing → $97 One-Leak Repair Sprint offer
5. **Purchase:** Complete payment → Receive exact fix implementation instructions
6. **Verification:** Implement fix → 30-day re-audit to validate change
7. **Retention:** Potential for additional audits or repairs

**Conversion Architecture Strengths:**
- **Low Barrier to Entry:** Free audit requires no signup or email initially
- **Immediate Value:** Results appear in under 2 minutes without account creation
- **Transparent Process:** Clear explanation of what audit checks and doesn't check
- **Evidence-Based:** Findings tied to actual page evidence, not generic advice
- **Prioritized Fixes:** Results ranked by priority to reduce decision fatigue
- **Clear Upsell Path:** Natural progression from free audit to paid fix
- **Risk Reversal:** 30-day re-audit included with Repair Sprint purchase
- **No Hidden Requirements:** Explicitly states no site access needed for audit or fix
- **Platform Agnostic:** Works with any publicly accessible landing page

  - One same-scope re-audit within 30 days included
