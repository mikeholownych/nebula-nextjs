# One-Leak Repair Sprint

**Price:** $97
**Stripe:** https://buy.stripe.com/5kQbJ1eawdj6eql1Jg43S0h
**Positioning:** One finding. One fix. Verified.

---

## What It Is

The highest-impact finding from the audit, converted into:
1. An exact fix brief (developer-ready work order)
2. Before/after screenshot documentation
3. 30-day re-audit to confirm the fix held

## What It Is Not

- A full site rebuild
- A retainer
- A vague "recommendations document"
- Nebula touching their code (customer implements)

---

## The Sales Conversation

The audit is free. The fix brief is $97.

**The moment that closes:**
They see the finding with evidence. The gap between "this is broken" and "I know exactly how to fix it" is the $97.

**What to say:**
> "Your highest-impact finding is [finding]. The evidence shows [measured value].
> For $97 I'll write the exact fix your developer needs - specific file, specific change, specific verification test.
> You implement it. In 30 days I re-audit the same selector to confirm it held."

**What not to say:**
- Don't sell the audit (it's free)
- Don't promise conversion lifts (you don't control implementation)
- Don't upsell to a retainer in the same conversation

---

## Qualification Gate

Only offer the Sprint if the audit found at least one finding with:
- Impact ≥ 4.0
- Quadrant: `quick_win` (low effort, high impact)
- Finding key: `headline`, `cta`, `above_fold`, or `social_proof`

If all findings are `major_project` (high effort), the $97 Sprint doesn't fit.
Acknowledge it: "The issues we found need development work beyond a quick fix. Let's talk about next steps."
