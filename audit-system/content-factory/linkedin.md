# LinkedIn Script Format

## The Formula

```
[LINE 1: Specific fact or number - no question, no "Have you ever"]

[LINE 2-3: Context. What most people do / assume. What's actually happening.]

[LINE 4-6: The finding. Exact. Measured. No fluff.]

[LINE 7-9: Why it costs money specifically for someone spending on ads.]

[LINE 10: The fix. One sentence. Actionable today.]

[LINE 11: Clear takeaway. Not a question. Not "what do you think?"]
```

---

## Worked Example (CTA finding)

```
Your "Get Started" button is costing you 40% of your pipeline.

I audited 47 landing pages last month from founders spending $1k–$5k on ads.
31 of them had generic CTA text - "Submit," "Learn More," "Get Started."

Average conversion rate: 1.1%.

The 16 pages with specific CTA copy - "Run my free teardown," "Get my conversion report" -
averaged 2.9% conversion. Same traffic sources. Same ad spend.

The user needs to know what happens after they click.
"Get Started" tells them nothing. "Get my audit" tells them everything.

Change 3 words. Retest in 7 days.

The difference between 1.1% and 2.9% on $3,000/month ad spend is $54,000/year in revenue.
```

---

## Voice Checklist

- [ ] First word is not "I" or "Have"
- [ ] Contains at least one number
- [ ] No banned words (leverage, unlock, synergy, seamlessly, transformative)
- [ ] Each paragraph is 1–2 sentences max
- [ ] Final line is a statement, not a question
- [ ] Could be read by someone who has never heard of Nebula
