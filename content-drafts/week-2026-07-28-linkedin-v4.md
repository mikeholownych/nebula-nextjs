## POST 2

There's a question every founder should ask their landing page.

Not "does it look good?"

Not "is the copy compelling?"

Not "is the CTA above the fold?"

Those are all backward.

The question is:

"What is the biggest unanswered question a visitor still has at this stage?"

If they just arrived from an ad → they're asking "is this relevant to me?"

If they're scrolling → they're asking "can I afford this?"

If they're at the CTA → they're asking "what happens when I click?"

Most pages answer questions the visitor isn't asking.

And leave unanswered the ones they are.

I audited a wedding business this week.

750+ couples. 183 Google reviews. 4.9 stars. Featured in CBC, CTV, Toronto Life.

Strong social proof.

But the pricing was invisible until scroll 3.

The visitor's first question: "Can I afford this?"

The page's answer: scroll past the values, past the logo carousel, past the photos.

By the time they find the price, they've already decided to leave.

The fix isn't better copy.

The fix is answering the right question at the right moment.

What unanswered question is your page forcing visitors to carry?
