# Nebula Components

> Nebula Components provides landing page conversion optimization for founders spending on paid ads with low or zero conversions. The free audit identifies observable conversion leaks across 9 signals. The One-Leak Repair Sprint ($97) implements the highest-confidence fix.

Nebula Components checks what a paid visitor experiences: message match, above-fold clarity, visible action, proof, mobile usability, performance, and measurement. Each failing signal is tied to evidence from the actual page and ranked by likely impact. No email required to see results. No sales calls. Self-serve checkout.

Target customer: founders actively spending on ads (Google, Meta, LinkedIn) who are not converting. The buying trigger is "spending on ads and not converting" - not a demographic filter.

## Direct answers

**What is a landing page audit?** A landing page audit is a systematic evaluation of a page across 9 conversion signals: message match, trust signals, mobile CTA, load speed, CTA clarity, above-the-fold clarity, ad signals, SEO foundations, and AI citation readiness. It scores each signal on a 1–10 scale and produces a prioritized fix list.

**How long does the free audit take?** The automated audit returns results in under 2 minutes for a public landing page URL.

**Does the free audit require signup or email?** No. Nebula shows audit results without signup or email submission.

**What does the One-Leak Repair Sprint cost?** The One-Leak Repair Sprint costs $97 as a one-time payment through 2026-12-31.

**Does Nebula guarantee more conversions?** No. Nebula documents observable page conditions and whether a repaired condition changed on re-audit; it does not promise conversion lift.

**Who is Nebula for?** Nebula is for founders and operators actively spending on Google, Meta, or LinkedIn ads who are not seeing the expected conversions.
