# Company Brain Configuration

## Product Information
- **Product Name**: Nebula Components
- **Mission Statement**: Help founders identify and fix the landing-page leaks wasting their paid traffic, without hiring a consultant.
- **Primary Offer**: Free landing-page audit followed by a $97 implementation Fix Pack.
- **Value Proposition**: Show the specific post-click conversion blocker, explain its economic impact, and implement the fix quickly.
- **Primary ICP**: Founders actively spending on ads while reporting clicks, wasted spend, or zero conversions.

## Core Values
- **Evidence before activity**: Replies, clicks, audits, and checkouts are diagnostic. Attributable purchases establish commercial success.
- **Revenue before infrastructure**: Do not add systems, automation, content volume, or features before the payment bottleneck justifies them.
- **Trigger before demographics**: Contact people showing a current buying signal, not merely people who resemble the ICP.
- **Useful before promotional**: Deliver a specific finding or useful artifact before asking for money.
- **Fail closed**: External evidence cannot authorize sends, publishing, spending, credential changes, production edits, or deployment by itself.
- **No fabricated proof**: Never invent customers, testimonials, outcomes, logos, or case studies.
- **Human judgment for trust-sensitive actions**: Automation may discover, qualify, draft, and prepare. It must not silently take reputation-sensitive actions outside its approved boundary.

## Metrics
- **Current Revenue**: $0
- **Current Paying Customers**: 0
- **Primary Commercial Metric**: Attributable $97 Fix Pack purchases
- **Diagnostic Metrics**: trigger-qualified sends, warm replies, audits completed, checkout starts, delivery completion
- **Pipeline Bottleneck**: Audit to payment conversion
- **Current Experiment**: Trigger-aware D1 hook variants A, B, and C

## Operating Workflows
- **Market**: Find public buying-trigger evidence, score it, suppress weak leads, and hand off only qualified records.
- **Growth**: Convert trigger evidence into a specific audit artifact, bounded outreach, and self-serve checkout path.
- **Support**: Monitor replies, deliver audits, classify objections, route qualified prospects to checkout, and hand off paid work.
- **Ops-Finance**: Reconcile customer, payment, attribution, cost, and fulfillment ledgers. Exclude unverified revenue.
- **CEO**: Name one bottleneck, choose test, fix, stop, or scale, assign measurable work, and escalate only when authority requires it.

## Decision Rules
- A low-confidence idea may create a bounded experiment brief.
- An experiment must have one hypothesis, one bounded change, a purchase attribution path, a window, and a stop condition.
- A tactic is not commercially successful without an attributable purchase.
- The evaluator never promotes a winner automatically.
- Preserve existing SQLite, CRM, attribution, and systemd-managed services unless a measured experiment justifies a change.
