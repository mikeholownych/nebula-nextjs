# Nebula Components Strategy Execution Map

Updated 2026-08-11 from the reviewed strategy framework and execution guide.

## Strategic Problem

Nebula has a functioning audit, offer, outreach, attribution, and payment system, but it has produced $0 in attributable revenue. Qualified attention is not yet converting into a paid $97 Fix Pack purchase.

If this remains unresolved, additional content, infrastructure, automation, and lead volume will create activity without a validated business model.

## ICP

Founders actively spending on paid traffic while reporting clicks, wasted spend, zero conversions, or a landing page that is not producing sales.

The trigger is more important than the demographic profile. A founder with a live conversion problem is in scope. A founder who merely owns a landing page is not.

## Strategic Position

Nebula competes through focused differentiation:

- Evidence-grade diagnosis of the post-click conversion leak
- Specific findings rather than generic CRO advice
- Economic explanation of why the leak matters
- Fast manual implementation through the $97 Fix Pack
- No consultant process or calendar requirement

This is a strategic hypothesis, not a proven competitive advantage. Competitive advantage is not declared until buyers pay and outcomes are documented.

## Shared Aspiration

First, produce and fulfill one attributable Fix Pack purchase. Then determine whether the same trigger, offer, and delivery path can repeat.

Success is not audience growth, reply volume, audit volume, or checkout volume. The first commercial milestone is a traced purchase and completed delivery.

## Primary Initiative

### Prove one repeatable path from a public paid-traffic trigger to a $97 purchase

This initiative is valid because it:

- Directly addresses the strategic problem
- Improves the buyer experience with specific value
- Creates learning about acquisition and delivery
- May reveal a defensible evidence and implementation advantage

## Resource Boundaries

- Use the existing audit, CRM, SQLite, payment, and attribution systems.
- Maximum outbound pacing remains approximately 25 D1 emails per day.
- No paid acquisition without approval.
- No new CRM, dashboard, content engine, or outbound platform before purchase evidence.
- Manual delivery is allowed for the first few customers.
- External sends, publication, spending, and trust-sensitive actions require the existing approval and verification boundaries.

## One Key Result

```text
At least one attributable $97 Fix Pack purchase with verified delivery.
```

Supporting metrics are diagnostic only:

- Trigger-qualified prospects
- Verified sends
- Warm replies
- Audits completed
- Checkout starts
- Delivery completion
- Objections and close reasons

## Execution Rules

1. Every action must state the trigger, hypothesis, bounded change, attribution path, observation window, and stop condition.
2. Eliminate actions that do not address the strategic problem.
3. Do not add actions merely because they are easy to execute.
4. Review the primary key result regularly.
5. Continue, revise, or stop based on the predeclared experiment rule.
6. Promote a tactic into a repeatable process only after attributable purchase and delivery evidence.
