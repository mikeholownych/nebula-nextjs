# Nebula Connector Strategy
# Based on Ivan/Lancer's connector distribution model
# Generated: Aug 9, 2026

## What a connector is
A connector is someone who:
1. Already has a network of Nebula's ICP (founders spending on ads, zero conversions)
2. Has high trust with that network (not just reach — trust)
3. Can naturally recommend Nebula's free audit as value-add to their audience

## Commission structure
- Free audit referral: No commission (coaches recommend freely, they look like the hero)
- Fix pack ($97) purchase: 25% = $24.25
- Pro subscription ($29/mo): 20% lifetime = $5.80/mo recurring
- When contacted by 10+ Pro customers through one connector = positive ROI for both

## Why this works better than cold email alone
- Cold email to 100 founders → 5-8% reply rate → 0.5-1% conversion = 0-1 customers
- One connector with 100 active students → 10-20% try the free audit → 5-10% buy fix pack = 5-10 customers
- Ivan closed $10K MRR in 3-4 months primarily through 2 connectors
